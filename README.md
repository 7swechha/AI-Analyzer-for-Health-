# FUME – AI Client Intelligence Platform

## Overview

FUME is an AI-powered client intelligence platform that analyzes unstructured client conversations and converts them into structured, evidence-backed health insights.

The platform is designed for health coaches, wellness professionals, and care teams who need to quickly understand client progress without manually reviewing every message.

---

## Features

- Upload conversations (.txt, .csv)
- AI-powered conversation analysis
- Overall Health Score
- Risk Detection
- Sleep Analysis
- Hydration Analysis
- Nutrition Tracking
- Stress Monitoring
- Exercise Tracking
- Mood Detection
- Medication Adherence
- Goal Progress Tracking
- Client Timeline
- Evidence-backed insights
- Confidence score for every prediction
- AI-generated coach response
- Human review workflow
- JSON Export
- PDF Report Export
- Email Coach functionality

---

## AI Workflow

1. Upload conversation
2. Parse conversation
3. Extract health-related information
4. Classify evidence
5. Detect health risks
6. Generate confidence score
7. Produce structured dashboard
8. Generate coach response
9. Export report

---

## Evidence Categories

The system distinguishes between:

- Confirmed Facts
- Client Reported Information
- AI Inference
- Missing Information

This helps reduce hallucinations and improves transparency.

## Hallucination Prevention

- Every insight is linked to supporting evidence.
- Confidence scores are displayed.
- Unsupported claims are avoided.
- Human review is included before taking action.
